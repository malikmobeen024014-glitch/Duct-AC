About Oasis Breeze

Your Partner for HVAC and Duct AC Solutions

Oasis Breeze is an HVAC and air-conditioning service company based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. We focus on providing practical cooling, air distribution, and ventilation solutions for residential and commercial properties.
